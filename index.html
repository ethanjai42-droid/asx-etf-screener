How to read this: Fit scores are a transparent screening aid based on the selected objective, horizon and risk comfort, plus broad exposure and management costs. The signal is BUY at 75–100, HOLD at 50–74 and SELL below 50. BUY/HOLD/SELL describes only fit to these filters; SELL is not an instruction to liquidate, and none is personal financial advice or a return forecast. Funds with overlapping holdings can add concentration rather than diversification. Check each fund’s current PDS, fees, holdings and target market determination before investing.
Issuer fund facts and return periods shown in expanded profiles are drawn from product pages/factsheets, generally as at 31 Aug 2026; holdings may be from a different date shown in the profile. Information and fees change. Returns are annualised total returns after fund costs where reported; brokerage, spreads and tax are excluded. This small starter universe is not every ASX ETF.
